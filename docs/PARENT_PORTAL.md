# 🌸 Parent Portal, Payment QR & Practice Videos Guide

This guide covers how to set up and manage the Parent Portal, Studio Payment QR, and Practice Video feedback loop in **My Piano Diary**.

---

## 1. Parent Accounts

Teachers create parent accounts directly from the studio. Parents have private logins and can only view their own linked children's lessons, attendance, tuition balances, and practice videos.

### How to Create a Parent Account
1. Go to **Students** (`/students`) in the sidebar.
2. Scroll to the **Parents & Portals** section below Families.
3. Click **New parent account**.
4. Enter the parent's full name, email address (their login ID), and set/generate a password (12 characters generated by default with the dice button).
5. Select which student(s) belong to this parent (supports siblings).
6. Click **Create Account**.
7. Copy the credentials from the post-creation dialog and share them with the parent.
   > **Note:** For security, the database only stores the bcrypt hash of passwords.

### Resetting Passwords or Changing Linked Students
- Click **Edit / Reset** on any parent card in the Parents manager.
- You can change their display name, reset their password, or add/remove linked students.
- To delete a parent login, click the trash can icon. Deleting a parent account does not affect the students or lesson records.

## 2. Studio Payment QR (Universal Banking & UPI)

Teachers upload their own payment QR **once**. It appears immediately inside the parent portal for parents to scan with their mobile banking app.

### Setting Up Your QR
1. Navigate to **Payments** (`/payments`).
2. In the **Payment QR** card above the monthly filter table, click **Upload QR**.
3. Choose an image file of your bank QR, VietQR, UPI QR, or QRIS code (PNG, JPEG, WebP).
4. The studio automatically downscales the image on a client-side canvas to under 512px / 300 KB for fast loading.
5. Add a **QR Title** (e.g. *BIDV Bank Transfer*) and **Payment Instructions** (e.g. *Please include student name + month in payment description*).
6. Click **Save QR**.

### How Parents Pay
1. Parents navigate to **Tuition & QR** (`/portal/payments`).
2. They see the QR card with your instructions and can tap to enlarge the QR to full screen for easy scanning with their banking app.
3. Parents send the money out-of-band and inform you.
4. You record the transaction in your studio payments ledger as usual. The parent portal updates in real time.

## 3. Practice Recordings & Teacher Feedback Loop

Parents and students can share home practice videos between lessons. Teachers review the performances in their studio practice feed and leave encouraging coaching notes.

### Two Ways Parents Share Videos
1. **Paste Link (Zero Setup, Free):**
   - Parents paste any link from **YouTube** (standard, shorts, or unlisted) or **Google Drive** ("Anyone with the link can view").
   - YouTube and Drive videos play in high-definition embed players right inside the portal and teacher feed.
   - Any other standard web link is displayed as an external video link chip.
2. **Direct Video Upload (Cloudinary Unsigned Preset):**
   - Parents upload MP4/MOV/WebM video files directly from their phone or computer (up to 200 MB).
   - Direct client-to-Cloudinary upload bypasses serverless body limits.

### Configuring Cloudinary for Direct Uploads (Optional)
To enable direct file uploads:
1. Create a free account at [Cloudinary](https://cloudinary.com/).
2. Go to **Settings → Upload → Upload presets**.
3. Add a new **Unsigned** upload preset:
   - Preset name: e.g. `mpd_practice`
   - Folder: `my-piano-diary/practice`
   - Allowed formats: `mp4,mov,webm`
   - Max file size: `200 MB`
4. Add these two variables to your `.env` (or hosting environment on Vercel):
   ```env
   NEXT_PUBLIC_CLOUDINARY_CLOUD_NAME=your_cloud_name
   NEXT_PUBLIC_CLOUDINARY_UPLOAD_PRESET=mpd_practice
   ```
5. Redeploy your application. The "Upload Video File" tab in the portal will automatically activate.

### Leaving Feedback as a Teacher
1. Go to **Practice** (`/practice`) in the teacher sidebar.
2. Filter by all students or select an individual student.
3. Watch the recorded performance in the embedded player.
4. Type your guidance, tips, or praise in the **Your Teacher Feedback** box.
5. Click **Save Feedback**.
6. When you leave your first comment on a recording, a confetti celebration appears! The feedback displays in the parent's portal immediately.

## 4. Route Security & Roles

- **Teacher Accounts:** Have `role = "TEACHER"`. Can access `/dashboard`, `/lessons`, `/students`, `/payments`, `/practice`, and `/reports`.
- **Parent Accounts:** Have `role = "PARENT"`. Automatically redirected to `/portal`. Strictly bounded by database relations: parents can never query or mutate data of unlinked students or other studios.
- If a logged-in parent navigates to a teacher URL (such as `/dashboard`), the layout guard redirects them to `/portal`.
- If a logged-in teacher navigates to `/portal`, they are redirected to `/dashboard`.
